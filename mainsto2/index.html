Prologue

On that day, I saw an idol for the first time in my life.

The first thing I felt was… light.

A brilliant radiance, as if something had just exploded.

Dazzled by the light, I yelled out like a fool, putting myself on guard.

And as I did―the idol standing atop that stage turned to look at me.

I may have imagined it, but it felt like our eyes met.

I wasn't in the audience seating, lit up with glow sticks, but rather, in a pitch dark passage where not a single light shined.

But even so, from upon that brightly lit stage, even though it shouldn't have been possible to see anything...

…that idol noticed me, and smiled at me.

And then, as he sang, he reached out in my direction.

But at that time, I had grown tired of everything in life, with no dreams or aspirations left―

Despite being in the dark, I felt resentment and envy towards those idols surrounded by radiance…

For just a moment, I disregarded the idol's beckoning gesture.

But after a second had passed, I couldn't help but reach towards that hand―

22-Starlight

…Alright, everyone. There doesn't seem to be anyone here, so you can come out now.

(Gasp) Jeez, I'm covered in mud! And these were my favorite clothes!

Fufu. I'm quite skilled at washing clothes, so I could remove the stains later, if you'd like.

Ugh… No, it's alright. Now that I take a better look, it's actually all just dust.

What even was that passage? It was so cramped and covered in spiderwebs—it was just awful! I mean, I'm glad we could escape safely, but still!

Speaking of which, are we not oddly high up?

It did seem as though we were going upwards as we went from place to place—but why are we at such a high elevation, if the stage from which we exited was underground?

Ufufu. You all are most likely unaware of this, but this building is actually full of secret passages.

I could follow you aaaall the way here while remaining hidden because I made good use of them.

Hmm. Secret passages, you say… my church also has a few, but isn’t the general public usually unaware of them?

Um, Mayoi-san, was it? Why is it that you know of those passages?

I'll tell you about that some other time… Or, rather, we can talk about all sorts of things once we've introduced ourselves again.

I was so desperate to get us out that I couldn't really afford to do so up to this point.

Yes. That will be necessary, as we all appear to share the same destiny.

…………

Umm… What's wrong, Hiro-kun? You've been quiet this whole time …

I thought you'd fail to read the mood like always and say something like "Yeah, let's introduce ourselves! Let's be friends!".

We've only just met, but even I understand that much about you.

Everyone―

I'm truly, terribly sorry!

W-wait, don't move so suddenly! It's dangerous! Our heads are gonna bump right into each other!

I'm so sorry! You all did nothing wrong, and yet I ended up dragging you into that turmoil!

Luckily, we managed to escape…but in the worst case scenario, I wouldn’t be the only one who would be subjugated for causing a disturbance in the live show venue―all of you would be punished with me!

Ah, I made such a fatal mistake, I couldn't atone for it even if I were to cut my stomach open! I'm unqualified to be your friend…!

Punish me adequately, Aira! Tatsumi-senpai! Mayoi-senpai! Punch me in the stomach with all your might!

You're being really dramatic, and your choice of words is as confusing as always… What does "subjugated" even mean?

But hey, it's okay, right? Since we managed to get here safely in the end.

That's right. If someone slaps you on the right cheek, turn to them the other cheek also. 1

No slapping, please! Violence is scary…! Being hit is a thousand times better than hitting someone, so please hit me insteaaad!

Fufu. Mayoi-san, you appear to understand the teachings of the Lord. Are you perhaps religious?

Eeeeek!? Someone like me? No, no, that's unthinkable!

This existence of mine is the furthest from anything holyyyyy! I'm sorry for being alive!

…Fufu♪

Oh, you're smiling. That suits you much better than a serious face does, Hiro-kun.

Though, I get how you feel― Right now I'm totally worn out, kinda ticked off, and in really low spirits.

I, as well. My doctor prohibited me from physically straining myself, yet I went against their admonition. Well, the situation did call for it.

But putting that aside… Although what matters most is that we escaped safely, What shall we do from now on?

Uh, usually I'd just want to go home. I'm totally drained.

It sucks that we didn't get to see Trickstar's live―we really can't go back to the venue just yet, can we?

Yes. I know I don't have the right to say this, but it really is unfortunate. Tenshouin Eichi said I would understand what an idol is once I saw them up close…

But just like that, I missed my chance. I really am no good.

Well, none of us four are any good, right?

We're irregulars―underachievers that can only lean on each other after having fallen into the deepest darkness.

But we're not alone, so it's not the worst it could be.

Fufu. Indeed, let's always look on the bright side. You see, for example… the stars are shining beautifully. Before we realized it, night had already begun to fall.

The stars… oh, they really are beautiful.

And as the sky gets darker, they become more and more so. For many years, the natural state of this area has been preserved without being exploited.

The starry sky you can see from here has always been beautiful, since there were no city lights and neither the air nor the sea were polluted.

But it's gotten slightly harder to see now, with the construction of the gigantic ES building and the urbanization of its surroundings.

Hm. Judging by the way you're speaking, Mayoi-senpai, are you a local?

That's right. I've always lived here.

It's a shame that the stars in the night sky are less visible now because the earth is shining so brightly…

…but that doesn’t mean these stars I love so dearly will just disappear.

…The four of us are just like those stars.

Yes, no matter how dazzlingly the other stars shine you can always find someone close to you, just like we are right now.

Yeah. Right now, we're the only ones looking at each other.

But someday, I hope everyone finds us… I hope they know. That even in the pitch black darkness, the stars―us idols―are there.

Thankfully, we were given a chance. The road ahead will be long and precipitous, but let us overcome it while thinking of it as a trial.

Fufu. Should we wish upon the stars? For us underachievers, us stars obscured by the light.

Yes. Once again―let's be friends, everyone.

…No. Let's shine as bright as the rest of the stars in the sky… let's become idols, together. 2

82-Quality

Liar! Liar! You liaaaaar!

Please calm down, Aira. I would never lie to a friend of mine.

But you lied to me! You liaaar, getting me to believe that you weren’t actually that capable all along!

All this time, you’ve just been laughing to yourself about how dumb I was for thinking I could be at your level!

You’re a traitor! So cruel, so cruel! Uwaaahhh...Tattsun-senpaaai!！

There, there. Please don’t cry, Aira-san. There is nothing to lament about this.

Fufu. It’s a wonderful thing, in fact. I first started to notice when I was teaching him the basics by demonstrating...

The level of skill that Hiiro-san possesses is quite extraordinary.

The speed at which he absorbs physical technique in particular is unlike anything I’ve ever seen.

Be it easy steps after one try or rather complex ones after several—he repeats them until he has memorized them, and then never makes the same mistakes again.

It’s quite fascinating. It’s too early to speak of his potential as an idol—but at least, he’s an extremely talented dancer.

Ugh...And compared to that, I’m...I’m at the bottom of the bottom of the barrel!

I spent so long talking about idols like some know-it-all, and then Hiro-kun just went and surpassed me like that!? My pride has been shattered! How am I supposed to recover from this!?

Don’t think of it in that way. It’s just that Hiiro-san is an unusual case—but you have a good grasp on the basics yourself, as well. You have been diligently striving to become better…

You’re aware that you won’t get things perfectly right on the first attempt, and so you consciously pick up on your own mistakes and improve steadily through trial and error—

But, that idiot! Even though he's an idiot, he just skipped the trial and error and got ahead of me in the blink of an eye!

He's a beautiful swan, and I'm just an ugly duckling!

I get it now! In this world there are geniuses and ordinary people, and the gap between them can never be closed—

Unfortunately, I'm just one of the ordinary people! But, hey, I thought Hiro-kun was just like me up until now!

And yet, and yet! Uuugh, did you ever even think for a second about the ugly duckling you left behind!?

It must feel so good to soar through the skies on your own without a care, huh!?

Hm, sorry...I don't really understand what you're saying, but it seems like I’ve hurt you.

What should I do? How can I make it up to you?

Shut up, don't be so kind! Don't comfort me, don't encourage me, don't try to make it up to me! Everything will just hurt me in the end!

W-what should I do, Tatsumi-senpai, Mayoi-senpai? What did I do wrong?

Fufu. It's not as if you’ve done something wrong, Hiiro-san.

Nevertheless, even at times when no one is in the wrong, and when everyone is in the right, people can still end up hurt and in tears. Please remember that, Hiiro-san.

Correctness does in no way equal salvation. On the contrary, it can hurt and torment people.

The history of humanity, in which war has been waged time and time again in the name of righteousness, is proof of such.

Mhm...I will carve this message in my chest, as it is surely an important thing to remember.

...Uhh?

? What’s the matter, Hiiro-san?

It’s nothing...a dog simply jumped in front of me. It doesn’t have a leash...I wonder if it’s a stray. No―for an undomesticated beast, its fur is quite well-kept.

It seems healthy and plump...it’s quite appetizing.

Huh? Did you just say appetizing? Are dogs considered food in your hometown…?

No, we don’t eat them. “Appetizing” is just a compliment used for animals.

There you go saying weird things again...Ughh, that funnyman act of yours even made me believe that you were some clueless barbarian!

You were just pretending to be an ignorant underachiever to deceive me! You betrayed me from the start!

No, that’s not it. It’s not like I—

83-Truth

Hey, hey~

...!?

Oops, sorry about my dog. Guess I should've put him on a leash~ this place is unfamiliar to him, so he's running all over the place to explore.

Hey, Daikichiii~ come heeere~ That guy’s not food, you know! Heeey!

You're—a member of Trickstar, aren't you?

Hm? Yup, I'm Subaru Akehoshi from Trickstar! And this here is Daikichi ☆

Ummm, where have we met before...Ahh, ehh, uhhh...Hold on, it's on the tip of my tongue...

This is weird...I can usually remember fans' faces just fine.

I’m not really a fan, but in last year’s—

Oh, I know! you're that guy I saw at SS!

I remember you standing in a weird place and looking really out of it. And you were glaring at us like you were really angry! Right, right...phew, I got it ♪

Thanks for coming to the show. I had a bit of an un-idol-like moment at SS, but still...

I really hope you enjoyed the show despite all that.

Mhm. I enjoyed it...I think. For the time that I listened to your song, I forgot about everything. About my birthplace, my brother, and even about who I am.

As a result, I was discovered by a security guard and caught for sneaking in.

Ahaha, so you snuck in. That's no good~ you have to buy a ticket. Money doesn't grow on trees, so watching the show for free is a little unfair to those who paid to be there, don'tcha think?

Mhm, I will keep that in mind. Putting that aside―

Since I happen to have crossed paths with you, there is something I would like to ask you about.

Huh? Something I can answer?

Mhm. I figured that you, of all people, might know.

—Just what are idols?

Hueh?

Uhhh...is that what you wanted to ask me?

Mhm. If you can, please do tell me. If I don't even know what idols are, I will never be able to grasp them.

That way, I’ll never fulfill my purpose—that wouldn’t be right.

Hmmm...I'm a bit confused, but I'll tell you if you wanna know. So you're asking what idols are—well, it's kinda hard to just explain upfront...

But! Basically, what you would call an idol, is me!

...

And you, too!

...Me?

Yup. You, and everyone! The whole world! Everything! All things sparkly and shiny!

That's what it means to be an idol! Simple, right? Do you get it?

No, I...don't think I do.

Really? Sorry, explanations aren't really my thing! Ahaha ☆

...Oh, whoops. I made plans for dinner, so I'd better get going. Nice talk, come see us live again sometime! Byebye~♪

Oh, alright... Thank you, I won’t forget the answer you gave me.

Cool! But mine isn't the only possible right answer, so why don't you think about it for yourself? Maybe everyone at ES, or every idol out there, is asking themselves the same thing.

What about you? What does it mean to be an idol to you? You came from the ES building, right? If you're an idol, we might be seeing each other often—let's be friends! What's your name?

...Hiiro. Hiiro Amagi.

I see, I'm Subaru Akehoshi!

Yes, you said that earlier.

Right. Make sure you remember, cause I won't forget about you either.

Mhm, neither will I.
