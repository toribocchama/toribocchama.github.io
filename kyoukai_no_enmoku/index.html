Translation Notes

  1. ↑ Phonetically, the title of the song is "Kyoukai no Enmoku", but it’s spelled with non-standard kanji, hence why the original title has katakana to guide the pronunciation. My translation of the title reflects this distinction by interpreting the kanji rendition according to the meaning of the kanji, and the katakana rendition according to the phonetics. "Kyoukai" has multiple homophones, but I chose "Program of the Boundary" because the word 境界 (pronounced kyoukai) appears later in the lyrics.
  2. ↑ The same distinction appears here as in the title of the song. The line as translated here is what is being said phonetically, but it’s spelled as in the title.
