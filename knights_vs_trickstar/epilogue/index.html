Chapter 1

The following day. Knight’s room in the Star House.

—What is the meaning of this!?

Eek!? Please don’t yell out of nowhere, could you? I was so startled that I messed up my make-up…!

What a racket… Suu-chan, what happened?

Where is Sena-senpai!? There is something I must discuss with him at once! But he’s not present…

Secchan? Oh… Now that you mention it, Tsukipii is also gone.

Oh, Izumi-chan said they had some business to take care of, and he left with Leo-kun to their place in Florence.

I see… I suppose it will have to wait until they return, then.

Huh? What’s got you looking so shocked at your phone screen?

Well, according to a communication I’ve just received from the office—

Up until now, Sena-senpai has been splitting his activities between Japan and overseas, as his modeling work is based in Florence…

… It seems that Sena-senpai has requested to switch to working exclusively in Japan hereafter, and the agency has agreed.

What…!?

Achoo!

Ohh… Sena, that means someone’s talking about you~

What are you talking about? Shouldn’t you be worried that I might’ve caught a cold, or something?

I didn’t, anyway. The wind is just strong, so there’s dust flying around.

We’re next to a big river, so of course there’s gonna be a lot of wind! Hyururuuun, run run run, it’s cause we’re by the riverside, hyurururururun ♪ 1

Why’re you singing that? Those are not even the right lyrics.

Don’t be a party pooper! Windy days have their positives!

Huh? What could possibly be good about the wind? My hair’s gonna get messed up, so I styled it for nothing. Why’d you ever be glad that it’s windy out?

‘Cause the wind is carrying a tasty smell our way~♪

(Sniff, sniff) Mmm, freshly baked bread!

Oh, you’re right. It smells nice.

Hey, since we’re here, we should get some to eat on the way home!

Huh? No way. Don’t you know that carbohydrates are a model’s mortal enemy?

Those who laugh at carbohydrates will cry over carbohydrates later! Put some respect on their name! 2

The show’s already over, so it should be fine to have just a little, right? Do it for me, at least!

…

Hah… Alright. I’ll make an exception.

… (Munch, munch)

So? What’d you think of my rec? It’s fresh out of the oven, you know! ☆

You were right, this store’s bread is pretty good. You buy from there a lot, right?

Yeah! Munching on these helps my writing flow~ I’ve written a ton of spectacular Bread Songs thanks to them.

Are you for real? Have they ever helped you write something not bread-related? Your clients are going to faint if you submit something like that.

… Wha—!? Hey, watch where you’re going…!

Are you okay? You almost knocked into someone.

Hah… there’s a lot of tourists. It’s really lively out today, huh.

But they’re standing in the middle of the road to take pictures and getting in the way. What a nuisance.

I mean, we’re right in the middle of a tourist spot… there’s that huge cathedral over there, so… 3

That doesn’t mean they have to make a fuss. They could always just not block the road and do their sightseeing quietly.

You were exactly the same when you got here! Making the rounds of every tourist attraction, making a huge fuss and taking a million pictures.

… Shut up. I don’t know what you’re talking about.

—Ugh, it's too crowded. Let’s take this back street.

Phew…

Finally, some peace and quiet. This neighborhood is so much better than those tourist spots… ♪

You really hate noisy places, huh, Sena?

I do, but that’s not the only reason.

Look; there’s colorful laundry hanging out to dry, that person is watering their flowerpots, and there’s a cat basking in the sun over there…

There’s something comforting about those markers of everyday life.

Oh, and I smell dinner being made~ ♪ Look, those people are cooking tomatos!

… Hold on. Those people look like they’re having a romantic moment.

I know I said the smells of everyday life are comforting, but isn’t that a bit too personal?

What are you saying? That’s what living is like. The smells are comforting because they’re signs of the people you’re surrounded by.

…

… I see. I guess the scene of this neighborhood ended up becoming a fixture of my daily life…

Translation Notes

  1. ↑ Leo is singing to the tune of Kitakaze Kozou no Kantarou (北風小僧の寒太郎), a children’s song (this part specifically). He swapped out the actual lyrics for his own.
  2. ↑ A play on the saying that “those who laugh at pennies will cry over pennies” (一円を笑うものは一円に泣く), meaning that it’s dangerous to underestimate the importance of money. Thus, Leo is suggesting that Izumi not underestimate the importance of carbohydrates.
  3. ↑ I can only assume this is in reference to the Piazza del Duomo (Cathedral Square), which indeed is probably the most visited area in all of Florence as part of its Historic Centre and the location of the Florence Cathedral.
