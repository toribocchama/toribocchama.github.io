  1. ↑ ES! continuity: just as he says, Yumenosaki’s theater club did perform (a very unique version of) Romeo and Juliet when Mao and Ritsu went to second year: Performance.
  2. ↑ In 1953 romantic comedy Roman Holiday, there is a well known scene in which the two leads ride through Rome on a Vespa scooter (Vespa being an Italian brand of motor scooters).

  5. ↑ While throwing one coin into the Trevi Fountain is said to make it so you will return to Rome in the future, throwing two coins is supposed to make it so you will fall in love in Rome. There are variants to this myth: sometimes it's about falling in love in general, and I read one according to which the second coin promises to keep you and your beloved together for life. Either way, throwing a second coin is related to romantic prospects.
  6. ↑ Ritsu is talking about two different meanings of 相手, which can be used for a companion or an opponent. He’s saying he can be Leo’s 相手 in either sense of the word.
