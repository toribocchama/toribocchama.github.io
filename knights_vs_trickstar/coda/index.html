Chapter 1

To sum things up… that is essentially what transpired.

Hah… so that’s how it is.

So it was then that Secchan left to practice alone, and Tsukipii was in such a shock that he went back to his room.

Precisely. To think that on top of the situation concerning Sena-senpai’s health, a new problem would arise in the form of Sena-senpai and Leo-san getting into an argument…!

To be fair, this whole thing also seems to stem from the issue with Secchan’s health.

Why is this happening? Ritsu-senpai, since your condition has finally begun to improve… if only Sena-senpai and Leo-san were in top shape, everything would have been perfect…

And yet, we are so misaligned that it’s almost refreshing… Well, I suppose whatever happens will happen…

Hey, now, don’t be so down. If this is how the King is holding up, what are your vassals supposed to do?

Anyway. From what I’ve heard, Izumi-chan is acting a little strange…

It appears to me that the task of juggling both of his current obligations is a daunting one, which would obviously hinder his peace of mind.

Hmm… it’s true that he has no respite in terms of time or pressure…

But even then, such a self-deprecating attitude doesn’t strike me as the usual Izumi-chan.

Is there some other factor that’s taking away his peace of mind…?

Though that may be the case, there is nothing we can do unless he decides to talk to us about it.

Yeah, true. For now, all we can really do is see what happens. If we butt into his personal life now, Secchan’s just gonna get more agitated than he already is.

… I understand. Considering that the second round is just around the corner, it would be preferable if the two of them were able to reconcile as soon as possible. Hah…

Hm? Where are you going, Suu-chan? Back to the room?

This entire matter has taken a toll on my mental energy, so I’ll be heading to the public bath to warm up.

Oh, I see. Take it easy now… ♪

Thanks for tagging along with my extra practice, Isara-kun. Since I was late to our group practice, I wanted to get a little more done.

Of course. In fact, I kinda felt like I hadn’t gotten enough exercise, so it worked out for me.

Ah, I’m relieved then~

It sounds cooler to dub it as “intensive overtime training”, but the truth is that the second round being so close is making me pretty anxious, and it’s more like I just wanted to distract myself with exercise.

Just doing some exercise makes me feel like I got something done, so I can rest easy even though we don’t really have anything figured out yet…

Yeah… we sure are practicing, at least.

But to be honest, I feel like I’m just going through the motions with no goal in sight. Still better than nothing, I guess.

Ugh, sorry about that. I’ve been looking back at our old performances for a bit now, but I still haven’t come up with any strategies that can help us with MM.

So much for the “brains of Trickstar”, huh… Maybe I really should give up my glasses…

Come on, you’re starting to sound like Subaru now. Glasses don’t magically turn you into a strategist. You don’t have to blame yourself like it’s your job.

It’s not just you that couldn’t think of anything. Neither could I, or Subaru, or Hokuto.

In our last practice, you raised the issue that we should come up with a strategy that takes advantage of Trickstar’s strengths, but we still couldn’t make anything out of that…

If anything, we’re all a bit useless for that~

Well, up until now we’ve always operated by rushing to act before thinking much about it.

Yeah, you put it well. Trickstar’s fundamentally a “go with the flow” kinda group of meatheads.

It makes sense when you remember the ones at the forefront are Hokuto and Subaru, the biggest meatheads you’ll ever find.

Last time we talked, I got the vibe that they weren’t really listening… Like the whole time, they were just hoping to get back to practicing ASAP.

Not that I don’t get Hokuto’s reasoning, I guess.

If we dedicate our time to coming up with a plan only to arrive at nothing, that’s essentially the same as wasting it.

Compared to that, even a second spent practicing is clearly more productive. So, I can see his line of reasoning.

And I also wonder if we wouldn’t have arrived at nothing all the same, even if we had put all our time into it.

I see your point…

(Like I thought, if I don’t come up with a concrete strategy before trying to breach the topic, I’d just be confusing everyone.)

(Hidaka-kun and Akehoshi-kun both seem to think that a good performance is a universal language, and that if we put on a good show, it’s sure to resonate with any audience…)

(... But, to be honest, that seems entirely too idealistic to me.)

(In fact, for everyone that’s participating in MM at all, ‘performing to our utmost’ is just a baseline requirement.)

(Everyone’s going to be showcasing the strengths of their own unit and putting their best effort into their performance, so we have to come to some conclusion if we want to win.)

(Performing well isn’t enough. We need to add something more to that.)

(Just like a game, putting all your technical knowledge into it is a given.)

(But who actually wins in the end depends on your strategy for party composition, routing, and which enemies to fight… there’s even cases in which luck plays a part.)

(To win against Knights… no, to move forward in MM, we’re going to need something that will allow us to capitalize on our strengths… some kind of weapon that only Trickstar can wield.)

…

—Heeey, Makoto. Are you listening?

Oh, sorry! I was lost in thought. What is it?

Hey, don’t stress over it, okay? If it’s not coming to you right now, maybe revisit the topic tomorrow and you’ll come up with a foolproof plan then. In the end, things’ll play out the way they’re meant to.

Yeah, you’re right.

Then, how about we head to the baths to give our minds and bodies a little rest from this whole thing?

Oh, sounds good. I was just thinking I wanted to do something refreshing… ♪
