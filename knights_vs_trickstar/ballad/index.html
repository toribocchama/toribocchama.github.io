Chapter 1

Let’s see. I think we’re good…? Oh, I almost forgot this. That was close.

Alright, perfect. Now, I just have to hurry—

Ugh… what the heck… You’re babbling on non-stop first thing in the morning… I was up almost ‘til dawn writing music yesterday, so shut up and let me sleep… freaking dummy… (Snore)

Sena-senpai…? Why are you making such a big commotion this early…?

Oh, morning, Kasa-kun. And why do you look all pathetic and scrunched up like a raisin this early? Do you even realize you’re an idol?1

L-like a raisin!? I just woke up, so it’s not like I had time to do anything about that… And what are you even doing…?

I told you, modeling practice for my show starts today.

Oh, right, you did say that. Good morning, guys~

Huh? You, up this early? What in the world could’ve caused this!?

Are we about to be struck down by lightning…?

I’ve been feeling pretty good lately. Maybe the day of my triumph against that pesky sun has finally arrived… ♪

You know waking up in the morning is the most normal thing ever, right? You’re only just getting to the starting line.

But, hey, that’s good to hear. Put those high spirits into practicing for MM.

Um, speaking of which. I’m not at all familiar with how the Modeling industry normally operates. Does this type of Show also require its own form of Practice sessions?

Not in most cases, but this one’s got eyes on it all across the globe, so Sera-san is hosting optional practice sessions. That’s where I’m heading.

Well, I’ve got to go. I’ll be back in time for our practice.

Good luck~

Goodbye… (Yawn)

No way… Izumi-chan is already gone?

Yes, he left just a moment ago. He said that he will return in time for Knights’ Practice.

I see… I wonder if he’s going to be okay. I really hope he doesn’t push himself to the point of collapsing somewhere…

Wait, Ritsu-chan!? Why are you up!? Do you know what time it is!? Oh no, did you stay up so late that your sleep schedule got messed up?

What the hell… So whenever I wake up early, everyone’s gonna pester me for an explanation? That’s annoying…

Dance studio, during Trickstar’s practice break.

Phew… Ah, I’m sweating bullets…

Agh! It got in my eyes…! Ow ow ow ow!? My eyes, I can’t see…!?

Who are you, M*ska? Here’s a towel.2

Is that you, Isara-kun?

Thank you, you really saved me there… I thought I was about to lose my eyes for good.

What’s with sweat getting into my eyes? I mean, my own body made it yet it’s attacking me, so I feel betrayed.

Oh, you’re drenched in sweat. You’ve been getting a lot of exercise, huh. You did seem extra pumped up for today’s practice.

Really?

That’s the impression I got, at least. Did something happen?

Hm… Maybe it has to do with the talk I had with Izumi-san yesterday?

Remember the show I got invited to model for? As it turns out, he’s going to be in it.

Huh!? For real? But didn’t you say it overlapped with MM?

Isn’t it gonna be a bit of a herculean task to juggle two events of that magnitude?

That’s what I told him, too, but he said he knows that and is prepared to take on both.

I think he’s definitely going all out for our match. It seemed like he was looking forward to going up against us.

We both agreed to do our utmost and compete fair and square.

Maybe that conversation subconsciously made me start feeling more competitive.

We’ve been friends for a long time, and he’s almost like a brother to me, but that’s precisely why I want to win. I’d like to show him how far I’ve come since we were little.

Oh, that makes sense. So you want to win against your childhood friend, huh? I can relate to that in my own way.

Alright, then! In that case, I’ll do my part as your unitmate and give it my all!

Haha, I’m glad to have your support… ♪

Chapter 2

At the same time, in a different dance studio. Knights’ practice.

Hello. Sorry I'm a little late.

Hm? Ohh, you’re finally back!

Didn’t take you for a latecomer, Secchan.

Welcome, Sena-senpai. We’ve only just begun our Practice, so there is nothing to worry about.

Actually, how’s your modeling practice going?

…

… It’s a good source of motivation. It's full of nothing but top models, so there’s a lot to learn.

I’m lucky to get to watch such first-class technique from up close. Any model should feel that way, at least.

…? Why’s there such a bitter vibe to what you just said?

…

Leo-kun. Which of these jobs came first…?

Huh? What do you mean, “first”? I don’t get it.

… No, it’s nothing. Now that I think about it, it doesn’t matter. Forget I said anything.

Huh? I don’t get you at all!

Can we stop talking about this and keep practicing? My time is too precious to waste right now.

Um… Sena-senpai, aren’t you perhaps tired after the busy morning you've had? If that is the case, wouldn’t you like to have a lighter session with us?

I agree. You must be exhausted from working so hard, right? Why don’t you listen to Tsukasa-chan?

I’m fine. Don’t mind me so much.

I told you I don’t have the time for that. I’m going to do things properly, as much as I can. If I didn’t, could I really say I managed to take on both jobs?

I talked about this with Yuu-kun already. If I do either job half-heartedly, I’ll betray his trust and embarrass myself in front of him.

Let’s just start already.

… As you say. Instead of reducing the intensity, let us take a short break and resume afterwards. Since you’ve just arrived, you should still wait a moment before starting.

Kasa-kuuun…? Are you listening to me…?

Please wait until I’ve finished speaking. There is a point to this. I want to dedicate the break to informing you all about the strategy we will employ for MM.

Huh, what is it?

After discussing with Ritsu-senpai, I’ve come to the conclusion that in a setting where idol culture is relatively obscure, it would be careless to approach this match as nothing but a matter of putting on the best Performance.

Yep. As idols, the global setting of MM doesn’t favor us. And that’s something every other unit will also be dealing with—

…But I don’t think most of them are thinking of it like that. So we’re going to use that to our advantage.

How so…?

Well, we have someone here with us that we can use to grab the international audience’s attention and direct it to us. Right, Tsukipii?

Me…?

You want to capitalize on Leo-kun’s international fame?

Exaaactly ♪

Hmm… but I’m only known as a composer, y’know? Who’s gonna recognize me as Leo Tsukinaga from Knights?

Well, there are people who know you as Leo Tsukinaga the composer, right?

What if they got to see a composer they’re familiar with singing and dancing on stage, and it turns out that he’s pretty good at it? That would get their attention right away, wouldn’t it?

Then, we funnel that attention into Knights, and it becomes much easier for potential fans to get into us.

That attention will be our advantage against all other units.

Precisely. Which is why I believe that the best course of action for this upcoming matchup will be to modify our plan so as to engineer the Performance around Leo-san.

Without making it extremely blatant, we should devise an arrangement that draws attention towards him subtly yet surely.

…

I see… Well, fine by me. If you wanna make use of the Leo from Knights, go ahead and use him as you see fit. He’s just some guy, though.

What the hell? You’re literally in demand. Would it kill you to act a little prouder?

Even if we can’t utilize Leo-san’s greatest weapon, let us arm ourselves with the weapon that is the Name Value of “Leo Tsukinaga”…!

Chapter 3

Some days later, after the first round of MM, in Trickstar’s changing room.

Ugh…

Argh, this sucks! We lost so baddd!

Hey, come on, we don’t know that yet. They’re not going to announce the scores at the end of every show, so it’s anyone’s guess until the end.

But… I’m pretty sure we lost this one.

I don’t need to see the scores to know. You get what I mean, Sarii?

Sure, we also got people hyped up, but it was clear that Knights did even better.

That’s… I mean, I can’t really deny that… but when you put it into words like that, it just feels worse. Like you’re speaking it into reality, I guess.

… “A master knows a master.”

Meaning that only those that have mastered a trade can instantly gauge the true ability of a fellow master. That also applies to this outcome.

Even if no numbers are shown, if we all have the same feeling that we lost, then that must indeed be true.

…

But this is only one loss. I don’t intend to lose again.

True, but what are we going to avoid that …?

Hmmm… I might just be stating the obvious, but we should really think of how exactly we’re going to make a better impression than Knights in the end.

A better impression… Like, practicing more and putting on a better performance than Knights…?

Precisely. If we continue as we have so far, we should arrive at that outcome.

This match helped me understand the present obstacle that makes it difficult to convey what makes Trickstar appealing and unique.

With that being the case, all we have to do is deliver an even better performance so as to mesmerize the audience.

Let’s start at once. I’ll go ahead and book a room for dance practice.

! Awesome! Let’s go, let’s go! Our light footwork is one of Trickstar’s strengths ☆

Let’s get changed into practice clothes right away. Come on, Sarii, Ukkii!

H-hey, wait a minute…!

…

(So Knights really went as far as to use psychological tricks. They’re taking advantage of every weapon at their disposal to ensure their victory against us.)

(They can’t use Tsukinaga-senpai’s music, and even then… with him at the center of their performance, they managed to grab the audience’s attention.)

(They got the audience to recognize the composer Leo Tsukinaga as the idol in front of their eyes.)

(It’s like Hidaka-kun said: just putting on a good show isn’t going to cut it.)

(Trickstar and Knights… There might be some subtle differences in our respective units’ abilities.)

(But those differences don’t translate into an obvious advantage. Our international audience isn’t going to notice something so minute.)

(They’re not familiar with idol culture, after all.)

(If you line up two masterpieces by different artists and ask which one is better, the answer you get will tell you more about the viewer’s perception than about the quality of either one.)

(But what if they had price tags attached?)

(In that case, the one with the higher price would make a better impression on the viewer, wouldn’t it?)

(That’s the psychological trick that Knights used against us. Since the audience is unfamiliar with idols, they added a price tag to help them understand.)

(So, even if we kept practicing until we were able to just barely surpass Knights in terms of ability, that wouldn’t help our score in the end.)

(Besides, it goes without saying that Knights themselves are extremely skilled. With just a couple days left, it would be impossible for us to practice hard enough to end up with an overwhelming lead on them in time for the second round.)

(What can we do…?)

Right, the same as always. I’ll ask Anzu-chan, and from then we can—

… Oh, wait, that’s out of the question. They did tell us that she’s working exclusively in management for this, so she’s not allowed to help or give advice.

(Now what? It’s not long until the next round. If we go into it with no clue on how to proceed, we’re just going to lose again…)

(… No, let’s not start panicking. At times like this, I have to keep it together…!)

(The others appointed me as the brains of Trickstar for a reason. I’ve got to gather some intel so I can get a grasp on the situation, and then analyze it.)

…

(Hmmm… Trickstar has plenty of experience in the industry, and we’ve won plenty of competitions before.)

(That’s right. Let’s think back to the times we’ve won in the past, and analyze how we got there.)

(If I can figure out how Trickstar managed to win all those times… I might be able to find out where our strengths lie…!)

… Hm? What? Subaru, stop!

Geez, what’s wrong? I’m dying to get changed!

As much as I’d like to, Makoto’s not with us.

Huh…?

Ahh! You’re right! Where’d he go? You think he got lost on the way? Makes sense, in a place like this…

Hm, I wonder… I feel like he hasn’t been with us since we left. Maybe he’s still in the dressing room?

For real? You think he just completely missed that we were leaving? He can be a little scatterbrained like that.

I’ll run back to the dressing room to check, and you go ahead to the changing room.

Okie dokie! I wanna practice a lot today, so come back ASAP!
